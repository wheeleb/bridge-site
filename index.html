About the photographs

From the low riverbank, a gray steel through-truss spans the water. The shores are rock and late-autumn color. Rapids move under the span, and in other views the same crossing is framed by evergreens or seen through tall pines.

What is visible in these pictures is all that is stated here.

History of the Crossing

The historic covered highway bridge on School Street crossing the Lamoille River in Wolcott, Vermont, was built between 1872 and 1874.

Key milestones in the crossing's history include:

  1. 1872–1874: Original timber covered highway bridge constructed to carry School Street over the Lamoille River.
  2. November 1927: The covered bridge was washed out during the catastrophic 1927 flood.
  3. 1928: Replaced by a steel pony truss bridge.
  4. 2026: Following replacement construction, the new School Street Bridge officially opened in October 2026.
